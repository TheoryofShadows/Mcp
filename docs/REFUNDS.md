# Refund Policy

**Effective 2026-09-12** · MCPX

## The short version

**14 days, no questions asked.** If a purchase did not work out, ask and we
refund it.

## How to request one

Open an issue at https://github.com/TheoryofShadows/Mcp/issues with the tool
name and the email you purchased with. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

## What happens

- The charge is reversed through Stripe to your original payment method.
- Funds typically appear in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
- **Your access to the tool is revoked.** Access on MCPX is granted by a
  non-refunded sale, so refunding removes it.
- The publisher's 85% share is reversed along with our 15% fee. Publishers:
  a refund is deducted from your connected Stripe balance.

## When we refund beyond 14 days

- The listing did not work as described and the publisher will not fix it.
- The install command was wrong or pointed at the wrong package.
- You were charged more than once for the same tool.
- The listing was removed for a policy breach after you bought it.

## When we may decline

- The 14 days have passed and the tool worked as described.
- Repeated refund requests from one account that suggest abuse rather than
  genuine dissatisfaction.

## Subscriptions

Monthly listings can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops future charges;
the current period is not pro-rated unless the listing stopped working.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of asking us, access is revoked
automatically when Stripe notifies us. We would rather you just asked — it is
faster for you and cheaper for the publisher.
